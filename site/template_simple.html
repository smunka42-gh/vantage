What the words mean

Below its usual price compares a price against its own recent history, not against other companies. It is not a discount: a business worth 40% less, priced 40% lower, reads exactly like an unchanged business priced 40% lower.

Quality is this year's verdict from six checks on audited annual filings — profit, returns, cash, debt, margins, revenue. Not enough history is not a bad score; it means too little filed record to judge at all.

Cheaper than usual compares today's price against what this company's own earnings have historically cost — never against other companies. A price can fall a long way and still be pricier than this company usually is.

How current this is. The quality checks read annual filings, so a verdict can be two to twelve months old. Prices are from the last close. The quarterly figures behind "profit still holding" carry their own date inside each card.
