How to read this

Below normal measures price against its own price history — how far it has moved from where it usually trades. It is not a discount: a business worth 40% less, priced 40% lower, reads exactly like an unchanged business priced 40% lower.

Shape dates the fall. The 50-day average catches up to a new price level in about two months; the 200-day takes a year. Both gaps wide means it is happening now; only the 200-day wide means it happened months ago.

Closest to failing is the one fact no finance site can give you, because it exists only relative to our own bars. {{ATRISK}} of this list {{ATRISK_VERB}} grazing a gate.
